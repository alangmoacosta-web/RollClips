Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Summary

RollClips is a personal desktop tool used by one person, the owner of the RollClips TikTok account. It does not collect data from other users, has no user accounts of its own, and does not sell or share data.

2. Data from TikTok

When the owner connects their TikTok account, RollClips receives, through TikTok's official login:

  • an access token and refresh token, used only to upload the owner's approved videos;
  • basic profile information (display name and avatar), shown so the owner can confirm which account they are posting to;
  • posting settings returned by TikTok (such as available privacy options), used to fill in the posting form.

3. How it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI). Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.

4. Sharing

RollClips does not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok, and only after the owner approves each post.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the owner uses RollClips. The owner can delete the stored tokens at any time, and can revoke RollClips' access in the TikTok app under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.

6. Children

RollClips is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version. Questions can be sent to the RollClips account on TikTok via direct message.
