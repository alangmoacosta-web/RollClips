RollClips

RollClips is a personal desktop tool used by the owner of the RollClips TikTok account to edit short vertical highlight clips of livestream moments and publish them to that one account.

The tool runs only on the owner's own computer. Every post is reviewed and approved by the account owner before it is uploaded through TikTok's official Content Posting API. RollClips is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's TikTok account.
